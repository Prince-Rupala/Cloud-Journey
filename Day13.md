## What Is a Loop?

A loop is a programming tool that repeats a block of code over and over automatically, saving you from coping and pasting the same commands.

## Difference Between for and while

For: Use a for loop when you have a fixed list or a specific count in mind before you start.

While: Use a while loop when you are waiting for something to change, and you don't know exactly when that will happen.
