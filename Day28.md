## What problem does cloud computing solve?

Without cloud computing if you made and a website, you need physical servers to run it, which can be costly. With cloud computing, you dont have to buy a server,you just rent their server for running your website and you pay a rent for as long as you use it, you can increase and decrease the servers based on your requirements which saves loads of money for the company.


## Why is renting servers often better than buying physical servers?

Renting a server gives you control of increasing and decreasing the services based on your requirements, suppose at 1st month of your business, you needed only 10tb of storage, so you just rent that muchand you can increase the storage as your business grows. Whereas, setting the physical servers requires a high upfront cost for hardware, space for servers, human resources, etc.


## What is AWS?

AWS also known As Amazon Web Services. It provides different types of services like virtual servers, storage, database, networking, etc over the internet.


## What is an AWS region?

A major location where AWS has data centres around the world like mumbai, singapore, tokyo, etc.


## What is an availability zone?

Availability zone means different data centres across a region like AZ-A & AZ-B.


## Why do regions have multiple availability zones?

Because if one data centres crashes, the app can continue running on different data centre.
